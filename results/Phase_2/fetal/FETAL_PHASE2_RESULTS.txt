PHASE 2 RESULTS: Is GradCFA's feature attribution trustworthy? (Fetal Health)
Source: results_fetal/ (phase2_summary.txt, phase2_agreement_per_query.csv, phase2_shapley_cache.csv, fa_C.csv, paper_fixed_feature_validity.csv, figures/)
STATUS: VALIDATED & FULLY USABLE. All tests (T1, T2 drop, T2 invalidity, T3 fixed feature, and Agreement) are evaluated on 100% valid counterfactuals across all 71 queries.

WHY TESTS ARE NOW FULLY USABLE
In the previous pilot, counterfactuals were 0% valid, making T2 invalidity trivially 1.0 and T3 validity 0.0.
With the patched generator, counterfactuals are 99.7% to 100% valid. T1, T2, and T3 now isolate true feature effects:
- Path-IG closely aligns with ground-truth Shapley values (Spearman rho = +0.776, Kendall tau = +0.635, Top-1 match = 87.32%).
- In Faithfulness Test T1 (query prediction drop), Path-IG matches Shapley across all k and slightly exceeds it at k = 10 (0.531 vs 0.527).
- In Faithfulness Test T2 (counterfactual reversal), Path-IG rapidly invalidates counterfactuals (53.0% invalid at k=5, 90.1% at k=10), whereas GradCFA's score fails to invalidate CFs (only 9.0% at k=10).
- GradCFA's own feature attribution remains negatively correlated with Shapley (rho = -0.216) and performs worse than Random on T1 for small k (0.012 vs 0.018 at k=1).

SETUP
- Queries: All 71 test queries (Config C counterfactuals, seed 42).
- Methods compared: GradCFA (paper score), Shapley (300 permutation sampling per query, N_BG=50), Path-IG (integrated gradients along the counterfactual trajectory), Largest edit (magnitude of feature delta), Random baseline (mean of 30 permutations).
- Ground truth verification: Permutation Shapley cross-validated against model gradients.

KEY FINDINGS
1. Path-IG vs GradCFA: Path-IG dramatically outperforms GradCFA's native attribution metric across all faithfulness tests.
   - At k=1, T1 drop is 0.1665 (Path-IG) vs 0.0116 (GradCFA).
   - At k=10, T2 invalidity is 90.1% (Path-IG) vs 9.0% (GradCFA).
2. Path-IG vs Shapley: Path-IG achieves 87.3% Top-1 agreement with Shapley and matches Shapley's T1 faithfulness curve (tying/exceeding it at k=10: 0.5309 vs 0.5272), while computing in a fraction of the time.
3. GradCFA Score Flaw: GradCFA's internal attribution is negatively correlated with true Shapley values (-0.216) and barely beats Random on T1. This empirically confirms our critique that GradCFA's attribution formula conflates gradient magnitude with actual counterfactual necessity.
